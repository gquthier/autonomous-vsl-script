# Beat map of a full VSL

The pacing of the deck this template was extracted from: a real 10:32 French VSL for a service business, 1,438 words and 104 slides, with the specifics removed. Use it as a rhythm to follow. It records structure only. No conversion data comes with it.

| Measure | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Words per slide | 14.3 on average, 24 at most |
| Slides with a diagram | 37 of 104 |
| Slides with giant figures | 18 |
| Slides with a named client | 8 |
| Text alone | 46 |
| Slides with an accent phrase | 68 |
| Part shares | 16.1%, 20.9%, 50.2%, 2.3%, 10.6% |

## Part 1: Intro, 18 slides

| Block | Slides | Beat | Visual |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | 2 | Names the audience. Asks the uncomfortable question about choice | none |
| Promise | 3 | Result and delay. "Without... even if...". Risk reversal | none |
| Pain | 5 | Respect, then the flaw. Two verbatims. Dated market fact, then the normal level | `bars` in two states |
| Proof | 4 | Who I am, with one self-deprecating touch. What I do. Total clients. Total revenue, sector named | stats |
| Plan | 2 | Where we are going. What you get at the end | none |
| Picture | 2 | Monday morning, the requests on the desk. "You can say no" | `board` in two states |

## Part 2: Old way vs new way, 20 slides

| Block | Slides | Beat | Visual |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Old | 4 | It used to be simple. The classic methods. What you were sold since. Platforms selling the same contact | `pills`, `fanout` |
| Why | 4 | All share one flaw: you wait. The buyer is doing something else. Their real path. Absent there, they sign elsewhere | `shift`, `journey` |
| Shift | 4 | "You know one": the newcomer. Reputation takes years. A system takes weeks. What they do, in three verbs | `curves` in two states, `numbers` |
| New | 8 | The image. The name and three pillars. Pillar 1 with the filter card. Pillar 2. Pillar 3 with the speed. "It sounds simple. Here are the questions." | `road`, `pillars` by state, `card`, `phone` |

## Part 3: FAQ, 53 slides, 10 loops

Each loop opens on a verbatim, turns the belief in one line, answers with the mechanism, and closes on one proof with its sector.

| Loop | Slides | "What is right" | Visual | Closing proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Done for my trade? | 4 | Honest: among the first, so the risk is ours | none | Stat |
| Unqualified requests? | 5 | You set the criteria, you may refuse | `filter` | Stat |
| Undignified, allowed, must I film? | 5 | The ad resembles your work. Nothing to film. Quote the rule | none | Stat |
| Already paid a platform | 5 | Installed at your place, in your name | `owned` | Stat |
| No time | 5 | What we do, what you do | `split` | Stat |
| Too short, wrong moment | 4 | Targets those ready soon. Live in days | `install` | Stat |
| What do I get? (offer) | 5 | Duration and complete stack in two beats | `stack` in two states | Stats |
| How much? (price) | 6 | Two amounts, paid to two parties, anchored on one contract | `twin` in two states | Stat |
| What if it fails? (guarantee) | 6 | Target, condition, where it is written | `deadline` in two states, `contract` | Stats |
| Free study a sales pitch? | 8 | One slide per deliverable, then the true deadline | `cards` lit one by one | Named client |

Note the rhythm. Slow, detailed loops sit at the end, where the viewer is deciding. The free offer is the last loop, so it leads directly into the CTA.

## Part 4: CTA, 3 slides

Button. Question. How the answer will be used. Diagram: `cta`.

## Part 5: Proof salad, 10 slides

Five named client results, two testimonials, two totals with their sector, then one line that repeats the action. Each result holds the screen for at least seven seconds. The button stays visible throughout.
